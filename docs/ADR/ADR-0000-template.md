## Problem

What forced a decision. One or two paragraphs; a reader who knows nothing else should understand
why doing nothing was not an option.

## Options considered

Each option that was genuinely on the table, with its real advantages — an option listed only to
be knocked down is filler. Include "do nothing / defer" when it was viable.

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly.

## Reasoning

Why this option won. Tie the argument to the project's stated values (determinism,
traceability, KISS) and to evidence or prior art where it exists — unsupported opinion doesn't
belong in an ADR.

## Tradeoffs

What we knowingly gave up or made harder. An ADR with no tradeoffs listed is describing a
decision that didn't need an ADR — or hiding something.

## Consequences

What becomes true elsewhere in the system because of this decision: constraints on other
subsystems, revisit triggers, migration notes.
